Financial markets glossary

Regulatory awareness and platform boundaries

This section summarises selected official SEBI materials reviewed for this prototype as of 2 November 2026. It is an educational summary, not a legal opinion or a complete compliance assessment.

1. Educational price data — 30-day lag
SEBI Circular HO/47/17/12(11)2025-MRD-POD3/I/11107/2026 dated 8 May 2026 prescribes a 30-day lag for both sharing and use of market price data for investor education and awareness, applicable from 1 July 2026. The circular also says market price data shared for these activities must not be accompanied by monetary incentives to participants. A one-day lag exception is stated for NISM's simulation lab only; this prototype is not NISM.
2. Education is not a licence to advise
Content must remain general and educational. Do not provide personalised investment advice, security-specific recommendations, target prices, buy/sell calls or claims about future returns unless the activity is lawfully undertaken under the applicable SEBI registration or permission framework. Whether a particular activity is regulated depends on its substance, not merely the disclaimer used.
3. Association with unregistered persons
SEBI's 29 January 2025 circular clarifies restrictions on regulated persons, market infrastructure institutions and their agents associating with persons engaged in prohibited advice/recommendation or return/performance claims, while clarifying the treatment of persons engaged solely in investor education. The platform should not market itself through prohibited recommendations or performance claims.
4. Social-media disclosures for regulated entities
SEBI's February 2026 circular on disclosure of registered name and registration number on social-media content applies to SEBI-regulated entities and their agents. Nav Yom must not imply that it is SEBI-registered if it is not. If the business later becomes a regulated entity or acts as an agent, the relevant disclosure requirements must be assessed and implemented.
5. Market-data licensing and redistribution
NSE's data policy states that subscribers must execute relevant agreements and comply with permitted use, handling, display and redistribution restrictions. A public website must not scrape, republish or commercially redistribute exchange data without the required rights and permissions.
6. No real trading in this MVP
The simulator is a closed educational calculator using synthetic prices. It has no broker connection, order routing, real funds, demat integration, investment advice, cash prizes or live price feed.

About Nav Yom Technologies

A new beginning for financial learning.

Nav Yom Technologies is a proposed mobile-first and web-based financial-education platform for young and first-time learners in India. It aims to make foundational financial knowledge more accessible through short lessons, quizzes, fraud-awareness scenarios and simulated portfolio practice.

The initial audience includes college students and early-career professionals, with Hindi and English as the proposed starting languages. The platform is designed to remain educational, transparent and platform-neutral.

🎯

Our objective

To help learners understand financial concepts, recognise risk and make more informed decisions through accessible education.

Our product principles

Clarity, responsible learning, data transparency, privacy-conscious design and no promises of investment returns.
